Assistant replies

A reply is one answer from the hosted assistant, the part of TobyAI that explains a computed result or a provision in plain language. The figures themselves are computed in code and are not metered: a payroll tax calculation, a reconciliation or a health check run does not use a reply.

Core includes 500 replies per seat per month. Most people use well under that. Past the allowance, each reply is 3 cents, billed monthly, or you can buy 1,000 replies for $25 in advance. If you connect your own AI through MCP, the assistant runs on your model and the allowance does not apply.

Common questions

What happens in the trial?

Everything in Core is available for 14 days from the day your organisation is created. A card is taken at the start through Stripe, and nothing is charged until the trial ends. Cancel before then and you pay nothing.

How are seats counted?

One seat per person who can sign in to your organisation, with a minimum of one. The subscription follows your member count: add a member and a seat is added, remove one and it is removed. Clients you act for are not seats: a bookkeeper on one seat can work across every client file.

How does the annual price work?

$540 per seat covers twelve months, which is ten per cent less than paying monthly. You choose monthly or annual at checkout. To move between them later, write to hello@tobyai.io and we change it at the next renewal.

Who holds my card details?

Stripe. TobyAI never sees or stores a card number; checkout and the billing portal are Stripe pages, and TobyAI receives only the subscription status.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time in the billing portal. Access continues to the end of the period you have paid for, and the free health check stays available afterwards. The record of what you ran stays in your account.
