What TobyAI is

What is TobyAI?

A compliance layer for Australian businesses and the accountants and bookkeepers who look after them. It holds the Commonwealth Acts, the state payroll tax Acts, the revenue offices' published rates and the FairWork awards as one versioned, cited body of law, and it computes what each obligation means for a specific business in auditable code. Every figure shows its working and cites the provision it rests on.

It is not accounting software, not a payroll system and not a lodgement service. It is the thing you reach for when the question is whether the figure your software holds is the one the law requires.

Who is it for?

Bookkeepers and accountants who need to stand behind a number for a client, and the businesses they act for, especially those employing across state lines. If you have ever kept a spreadsheet of thresholds, rates and due dates beside your accounting software, TobyAI replaces the spreadsheet and adds the citation.

Is this tax, legal or financial advice?

No. TobyAI states what the legislation, the revenue office or the award provides, and what your figures compute to under it. That is information, not tax, legal, financial or employment advice, and the Terms of Use say so. A qualified person decides what to do with it. For a registered agent that means TobyAI does the finding and the arithmetic; the professional judgement, and the sign-off, stay with you.

How is this different from asking a chatbot, or searching the ATO site?

A general chatbot writes the number. TobyAI never does. Rates, thresholds, dates and caps live in versioned schedules that name their source and the date they were checked, and the engine computes from those in code. AI is used only to explain a computed result in plain language and to help you frame the question; it cannot change a figure, add a rate or take a legal position.

Searching the ATO or a revenue office finds you the page. TobyAI finds the provision, applies it to your figures, tells you which version of the law it used and gives you a record you can file. And where it does not hold the source, it says so rather than filling the gap.

Working with Xero, MYOB and payroll software

Is TobyAI a replacement for Xero or MYOB?

No. TobyAI keeps no ledger and writes nothing to yours. It reads figures from your accounting file or an export and checks them against the law in force on the date in question. Keep your accounting software; TobyAI sits beside it and does the part it was never built to do.

Does TobyAI run payroll?

No. It checks that the super guarantee rate, the PAYG scale, the payroll tax threshold, the due dates and the award rates your payroll uses are the ones the law provides on that pay date, and shows where the law says so. Paying people stays with your payroll system.

Does TobyAI lodge with the ATO or a revenue office?

No. TobyAI prepares and checks. You or your registered agent lodge, as you do today.

What does TobyAI read from Xero, and can it change anything?

Read-only accounting data, authorised by you in Xero, read server side in Sydney. The free health check reports what in the file would not survive review, with each finding cited. TobyAI never writes to your Xero file, and you can revoke its access in Xero at any time.

My clients use MYOB, QuickBooks or another payroll system. Does it still work?

Yes, by file. Export the report or payroll data from the software and attach it in the portal; the same engine audits it against the same sources and shows each finding with its citation. Xero connects directly today; direct connections to other ledgers and payroll systems follow.

How does it fit into a bookkeeping or accounting practice?

As the check before the figure goes out. Run a client's payroll period, BAS or year-end figures through it and get back the items that do not match the law in force, each with the provision, the working and the schedule version. That is the file note. Clients are served from the same portal, and the answer they receive is the one you would have had to look up.

Where the figures come from

Where does a number come from?

From a versioned schedule that names its source (the Act, the ATO page, the revenue office page, the Fair Work Commission's data) and the date it was checked, attested by a named Compliance Lead before it is used. The engine reads the schedule and computes the figure with the working shown in cents. You can read the schedule and check it against the source yourself.

What does AI actually do, then?

It explains the computed answer in plain language and helps you ask the right question. It never originates a rate, threshold, date, classification or legal position, and the engine that computes figures contains no AI at all. That boundary is enforced in code and tested on every change, not stated as a policy.

How do I know a figure is right?

Three ways. The working is shown, so you can follow it. The source is cited and linked, so you can read the provision or the published rate yourself. And the engine is held to the worked examples the publishers print, so a Revenue NSW or SRO Victoria example computes to the publisher's own answer before a change ships. Each answer also states whether the schedule behind it is attested.

What about questions that are a judgement call?

Employee or contractor, whether entities are grouped, whether a position is reasonably arguable: TobyAI surfaces the test and its citation and stops. A person records the decision, and the evidence they saw, before any figure is computed on it. The decision is logged as an input, never inferred or pre-filled.

What happens when TobyAI does not have the answer?

It says so. A state it does not compute, a rate the ATO has not yet published, a compilation it does not hold for the date asked: each is refused, or handed back with the test and the citation, rather than filled with an estimate that looks right.

The law it holds

Which law does TobyAI hold?

Commonwealth tax and employment legislation from the Federal Register, the payroll tax Acts of every state and territory, the revenue offices' published rates and thresholds, ATO guidance and rulings, and the FairWork awards, as one indexed corpus with every paragraph traceable to its publisher. Consolidated legislation is held as the published compilations, so a citation points at the text as it stood, not a paraphrase. What is computed, and what is held for research only, is set out on the coverage pages.

Can I ask about a prior year?

Yes. Ask as at a date and TobyAI answers on the law in force then, naming the compilation or schedule it used. Amended returns, audits and late lodgements are answered on the law of the day, not today's rates.

How current is it?

Legislation and published rates are refreshed on a schedule against their publishers, and each document carries the date the publisher printed and the date it was retrieved. A change to a figure goes through the Compliance Lead's attestation before the engine uses it. Where a publisher has not yet printed a figure, such as a quarterly interest rate the ATO has not released, TobyAI says the figure is not yet held.

Which states and territories are covered?

Commonwealth law applies everywhere. Payroll tax computes for Queensland, New South Wales and Victoria today; the other states' and territories' Acts are held for research, and a question that needs a figure for one of them is refused rather than approximated.

Data and security

Where is my data, and my clients' data?

Processed and held in Sydney. No name, tax file number, bank detail or salary reaches an AI provider; identifiers are hashed before anything leaves the deterministic layer. Nothing you send is used to train a model. The privacy policy sets this out.

Who can see my organisation's data?

The people you add to your organisation, and no other customer. Sign-in is through your identity provider, and the API refuses any tool call until the current Terms of Use have been accepted by your organisation.

Can I keep a record of what it told me?

Yes. Each answer carries the schedule version, the source, the provision and a fingerprint of the assessment, so a file note can be reconstructed later and the provision reread as it stood.
